About Deboss Footwear

Deboss Footwear is a handcrafted footwear brand dedicated to creating stylish, comfortable and quality shoes for every occasion.

From classic designs to custom-made requests, we focus on creating footwear that reflects our customers' unique style.

Our Story

Established in 2017, Deboss Footwear started with a passion for creating quality handmade shoes. Over the years, the brand has continued to grow by providing customers with carefully crafted footwear designed with attention to detail.

Located at Kalma Street, Federal Housing, Gonin Gora, Kaduna, Deboss Footwear creates different types of shoes including boots, cover shoes, sneakers, slippers, belts and custom designs.
